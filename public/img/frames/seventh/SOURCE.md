# Seventh Edition frame assets

The ordinary frames and Goudy Medieval font were copied from the local [Card Conjurer](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer) clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12`. Asset choices and geometry follow `js/frames/packSeventh.js`. Special overlays, alternate lands, and masks are excluded. Dual colors use the family's multicolored frame.

The clone has no explicit license file for these assets. Its `legal/index.html` says Magic-related material belongs to Wizards of the Coast and is intended for personal, non-commercial use. This note is attribution, not a license grant.
