# M15 frame assets

These frame images, power/toughness backgrounds, and masks were copied from the local Card Conjurer clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12` ([repository](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer)). The Box Topper frame definitions are in `js/frames/packM15BoxTopper.js`; M15 Regular comes from `js/frames/packM15RegularNew.js`; M15 Extended Art from `js/frames/packM15ExtendedArtNew.js`; Borderless from `js/frames/packBorderless.js`; the full-resolution Snow frames under `new/snow/` from `js/frames/packSnowNew.js`; Nyx frames under `new/nyx/` from `js/frames/packM15NyxNew.js`; and Universes Beyond regular frames under `new/ub/` with P/T assets under `ub/pt/` from `js/frames/packUBNew.js`. Snow, Nyx, and Universes Beyond reuse the M15 New masks; optional watermarks, crowns, stamps, and enchantment variants are not part of these ordinary-card families. The accurate Universes Beyond pack references a missing colorless frame, so this family falls back to Artifact for colorless. Borderless's pinline mask is defined by `js/frames/packGenericShowcase.js`. The masks and two-color layering behavior are described in `js/autoFrame.js` and `js/creator-23.js`.
