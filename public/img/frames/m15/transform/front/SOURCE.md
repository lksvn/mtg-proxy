# M15 Transform front

Frame PNGs and composition masks copied from the local CardConjurer source:
`img/frames/m15/transform/regular/`, defined by `js/frames/packM15TransformFront.js`.

Source images are scaled to the editor's 1500 × 2100 canvas. P/T boxes and the
type/right-half masks reuse existing M15 assets. The front arrow is part of the
frame. This step adds the ordinary front frame only, not paired-face editing or
custom transform icons.
