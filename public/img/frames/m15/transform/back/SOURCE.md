# M15 Transform back

Frame PNGs and masks copied from the local CardConjurer source:
`img/frames/m15/transform/regular/new/`, defined by
`js/frames/packM15TransformBackNew.js`.

Dark P/T boxes come from `img/frames/m15/transform/regular/`.
Rules, type, and right-half masks reuse existing M15 assets. Images scale to
the editor's 1500 × 2100 canvas. The back arrow is baked into the frame.
The color indicator reuses the Planeswalker indicator and reads the mana cost;
the cost itself is not printed. Paired-face editing and icon selection remain
separate steps.
