# Fourth-era frame assets

The eight full-size ordinary frames and black-border overlay were copied from the local [Card Conjurer](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer) clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12`.
