# Old Borderless frame assets

The ordinary full-size frames were copied from Card Conjurer's "Old Floating" pack in the local [Card Conjurer](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer) clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12`. Asset choices and geometry follow `js/frames/packOldFloating.js`. The short-text variation, special overlays, and composition masks are excluded. Dual colors use this family's multicolored frame.
