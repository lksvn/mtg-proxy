# ABU frame assets

The full-size ordinary frames, border mask, dual-land mask (`dualLand.png`), five old-style colored mana symbols (`img/manaSymbols/old/old*.svg`), and old tap symbol (`img/manaSymbols/oldtap.svg`) were copied from the local [Card Conjurer](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer) clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12`. Asset choices and geometry follow `js/frames/packABU.js`. Thumbnails are excluded; unsupported variants fall back within ABU.
