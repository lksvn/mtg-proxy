# ABU frame assets

The full-size ordinary frames, border mask, dual-land mask (`dualLand.png`), five old-style colored mana symbols (`img/manaSymbols/old/old*.svg`), and historical tap symbols (`img/manaSymbols/oldtap.svg` and `img/manaSymbols/originaltap.svg`) were copied from the local [Card Conjurer](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer) clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12`. Asset choices and geometry follow `js/frames/packABU.js`. The Revised family reuses these frame assets with a white border and the tilted-T `originaltap.svg`; it is an approximation until dedicated Revised scans are available. Thumbnails are excluded; unsupported variants fall back within their selected family.

The clone has no explicit license file for these assets. Its `legal/index.html` says Magic-related material belongs to Wizards of the Coast and is intended for personal, non-commercial use. This note is attribution, not a license grant.
