# Regular token frame assets

These full-resolution regular, tall, textless, Nyx, and Original old-border token frames were copied from the local Card Conjurer clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12` ([repository](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer)). The M15 frame definitions and text geometry are in `data/scripts/versions/token/`; the Original old-border definitions are in `js/frames/packTokenOld.js`. Emblem, marker, and other utility treatments are not included.
