# Regular token frame assets

These full-resolution regular, tall, short, textless, textless-borderless, Nyx, Original old-border, Unglued, Monarch, Marker, and Initiative token frames were copied from the local Card Conjurer clone at `D:\www\cardconjurer`, revision `d3c6706692898d596ec6a5be0be44f63062c9e12` ([repository](https://github.com/joshbirnholz/cardconjurer)). The M15 frame definitions and text geometry are in `data/scripts/versions/token/`; additional definitions are in `js/frames/packTokenShort-1.js`, `js/frames/packTokenTextlessBorderless.js`, `js/frames/packTokenOld.js`, `js/frames/packTokenUnglued.js`, `js/frames/packTokenMonarch.js`, `js/frames/packTokenMarker.js`, and `js/frames/packTokenInitiative.js`. Emblem and other remaining utility treatments are not included.
